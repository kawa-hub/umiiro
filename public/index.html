The seas of Japan

Seas that feel like
different countries.

The Japanese archipelago stretches about 3,000 km from north to south. Coral reefs spread around the southern islands, while drift ice reaches the northern coast each winter. The warm Kuroshio and the cold Oyashio currents meet offshore, so the sea changes character with every place and season. Umiiro collects some of them in one place.
